    Group BRally

    No speed limit on the stages. One special stage after another on roads that never repeat, across gravel, tarmac and snow, until the car or the clock gives out.

    Group B car
    Rules
    • Special stages. Each stage is timed from the start line to the flying finish. Stage times add up, and the overall classification decides who leads the rally.
    • Start control. A five-second count. Crossing the line early is a jump start, +10 s. Hold gas and brake together to spool the turbo on the line.
    • Pace notes. The co-driver calls each corner by severity, 1 tightest to 6 nearly flat, plus hairpins, crests, jumps and surface changes.
    • Damage carries over. Trees, rocks, hard landings and rough ground cost power, then add a steering pull. 100% means retirement.
    • Service. A roadside fix after every stage, a full service park after every third.
    • Maximum time. Every stage has one. Go over it and you are out of time and excluded. Off and stuck? Spectators push you back on, +10 s.

    GrandPrix

    This season's calendar, 23 rounds as raced, against eleven teams of two. You take the second seat in the team you choose; every car has the same engine, so the team is your colours and your teammate.

    Team

    Rules
    • Standing start. Five red lights come on one by one, then go out. Moving before they go out is a jump start, +5 s.
    • Tyres. Soft is fastest and wears quickest, hard lasts longest. Worn tyres lose grip. A full race requires a stop and a second compound or you are disqualified; sprints need no stop.
    • Pit stops. Press B (or BOX) and you pit as you cross the line. A stop costs about eighteen seconds.
    • Track limits. All four wheels over the white line counts. Three warnings, then +5 s for each one after.
    • DRS. Enabled once the leader has done two laps. Be within one second of the car ahead at a detection line and you can open the rear-wing flap in the next zone with Shift (or DRS): about 12 km/h more top speed, but less rear grip. Braking or the end of the zone shuts it.
    • The car. Built on real figures: 0–100 km/h in about 2.7 s, 0–200 in 5.2 s, 330 km/h flat out, around 5 g on the brakes from top speed and up to 5 g in fast corners, but only about 2.3 g in slow ones. The rear is planted; it only lets go if you badly overcook a corner or floor it at full lock in a hairpin.
    • Points. 25-18-15-12-10-8-6-4-2-1 for the top ten; sprints pay 8 down to 1 for the top eight.
